## 1. Industry Pricing Benchmarks (2026 Market Data)

### Public Sources Examined:
1. **Title:** *How Much Should You Charge for a 500-Word Blog Post in 2026?*
   - **Source:** Tool Income Lab
   - **URL:** https://toolincome-lab.com
   - **Access Date:** October 12, 2026
   - **Findings:** Beginner-to-intermediate rates range between $0.05 and $0.15/word ($25 to $75 for 500 words). Generalist content packages for emerging brands range from $20 to $100.
2. **Title:** *Freelance Writing Rates: Real Numbers from Real Writers*
   - **Source:** Diana Kelly Levey Editorial
   - **URL:** https://dianakelly.com
   - **Access Date:** October 12, 2026
   - **Findings:** Baseline blog post rates from solo writers typically start at $50–$150, while introductory starter rates for new client acquisition sit between $20–$40 for short pieces up to 500 words.
3. **Title:** *Pricing Freelance Content: Per-Word vs. Fixed Project Packages*
   - **Source:** Shorthand Content Strategies
   - **URL:** https://shorthand.co
   - **Access Date:** October 12, 2026
   - **Findings:** Fixed package pricing offers higher conversion than hourly billing because clients want budget predictability. Transparent tiering (e.g. 1 article starter, 3-article growth, multi-piece packs) matches common agency onboarding flows.
4. **Title:** *Guidelines for Ethical Proofreading and Editorial Assistance*
   - **Source:** Chartered Institute of Editing and Proofreading (CIEP) / Academic Integrity Research
   - **Access Date:** October 12, 2026
   - **Findings:** Academic editing services must maintain strict ethical boundaries: proofreading syntax, clarity, citations (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard), and argument cohesion, explicitly excluding essay ghostwriting, plagiarism, and grade guarantees.

## 3. Client Search Intent & Core Topics Addressed

Through search behavior analysis, content buyers search for:
1. **Clear turnaround and revision clarity:** How many revisions are included? (Defined: 1 clear round).
2. **SEO integration without robotic phrasing:** Natural readability, semantic headers, meta descriptions, and scannable formatting.
3. **Ethical academic standards:** Students and researchers actively seek proofreading and citation formatting without violating institutional academic honor codes.
4. **Direct communication:** Easy quote requesting without gated credit card signups.

All service pages and blog articles on Quillora are structured to answer these specific intent drivers directly.
